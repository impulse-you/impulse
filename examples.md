# Things to ask Impulse

Impulse is your personal AI assistant that learns your routine and gets things done for you, often before you ask. You text it the way you would text a friend. These are the kinds of messages people send it, grouped by what they are for.

## Money back

- My food order came an hour late. Get me a refund.
- My flight was delayed by four hours. Claim whatever I am owed.
- Find any subscriptions I am not using and cancel them.
- Check my recent orders for price drops and ask for the difference back.

## Travel

- Get me on an earlier flight home today.
- Book train tickets to see my parents next weekend.
- Check me in for my flight tomorrow.
- Find a hotel near the conference for three nights, under 200 a night.

## Food and shopping

- Reorder my usual groceries for tomorrow morning.
- Order the ingredients for this recipe.
- Send my sister a birthday gift that arrives by Sunday.
- Tell me when these shoes come back in my size.

## Bookings and appointments

- Book a table for four on Friday night, somewhere quiet.
- Move my dentist appointment to Thursday.
- Book a tennis court for Saturday morning.
- Find a plumber who can come on Friday.

## Bills and forms

- Pay my electricity bill before it is due.
- Fill in my passport renewal and show me before you submit it.
- Renew my car registration.

## Keeping on top of things

- Send me a short summary of my day every morning.
- Remind me to call my mother on Sunday.
- Keep chasing the landlord until the boiler is fixed.

Impulse checks with you before anything is paid for. More at [impulse.you](https://impulse.you).
