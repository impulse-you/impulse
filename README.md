# Impulse

**Your personal assistant that gets things done for you.** Impulse is also known as Impulse AI and Impulse assistant.

Website: [impulse.you](https://impulse.you) · [Terms](https://impulse.you/terms) · [Privacy](https://impulse.you/privacy)

## What is Impulse?

**Impulse is your personal AI assistant that learns your routine and gets things done for you, often before you ask. Just text it on iMessage, WhatsApp, SMS or Telegram, or call it.**

There is no new app to learn. You text Impulse the way you would text a friend, or send a voice note, and it does the work. Impulse has its own phone number, its own email address and its own computer, and it uses them the way you would: it opens websites, signs in, fills in forms, makes the booking, places the order and follows up until the job is finished. Then it texts you that it is done.

Impulse is opening soon at [impulse.you](https://impulse.you).

Not the Impulse you were looking for? [Impulse Labs](https://impulselabs.ai) at impulselabs.ai is an unrelated platform for building machine learning models. Impulse CRM is an unrelated customer relationship tool for sales teams. This repository is about Impulse the personal assistant at impulse.you.

## What is Impulse AI?

**Impulse AI is the personal assistant at [impulse.you](https://impulse.you): an AI you text, which acts on your behalf.** It is not a chatbot that only answers questions. When you ask it to do something, it does it, and it checks with you before anything is paid for.

Impulse AI is the same product as Impulse. You never pick a model or write a special prompt. You say what you need in your own words, in the chat app you already use.

## Why impulse.you?

Because it's your impulse. You have the impulse (book the table, chase the refund, send the gift) and Impulse acts on it. The dot in the logo makes the same point: it hops from the *i* to the *m* the moment the impulse becomes action.

## How Impulse works

1. **Text it.** Tell Impulse what you need on iMessage, WhatsApp, SMS or Telegram, by text or by voice note. You can also call it.
2. **It does the work.** Impulse uses its own phone, email and computer to get the task done, on the websites and services you already use.
3. **It asks before it pays.** Before any purchase, Impulse shows you what it is buying, from whom, and the total, and waits for your yes.
4. **It tells you when it is done.** You get a text with the result: the booking reference, the order, the refund.
5. **It learns your routine.** Impulse remembers your preferences and how you like things done, so it can handle more on its own over time, often before you ask.

## What can Impulse do?

Impulse can reorder your groceries, book your flight tickets, pay your bills on time, get you a refund, or book a table for Friday night. Some things it gets done in a day:

- A birthday gift sent to your sister
- A dentist appointment moved to Thursday
- A tennis court booked for Saturday
- An unused subscription cancelled
- A passport renewal submitted
- Money back for a delayed flight
- A plumber booked for Friday
- Weekend train tickets bought

More examples of things to ask Impulse are in [examples.md](./examples.md).

## Who is Impulse for?

Impulse is for anyone with more to do than time to do it: busy professionals, parents running a household, people who travel, and anyone with a list of small jobs they keep putting off. If you can send a text, you can use Impulse.

## Does Impulse need an app?

No. Impulse works inside the messaging apps you already have: iMessage, WhatsApp, SMS and Telegram. You can also email it or call it. There is nothing to download.

## Is Impulse safe to use?

Impulse is built to act for you without acting behind your back:

- **It asks before it spends.** Every purchase waits for your yes.
- **Your logins stay in a vault.** When Impulse needs to sign in somewhere, it uses details you saved in your private vault. You never type a password into the chat.
- **You decide what it can see.** You choose which accounts to connect, and you can disconnect them or delete your data at any time.

Read the [privacy policy](https://impulse.you/privacy) and the [terms](https://impulse.you/terms) for the details.

## Is Impulse an Instinct alternative?

Impulse belongs to the same new kind of personal assistant as Instinct, Poke and Meta's Muse: assistants you text, which then act for you on the web, rather than chatbots that only reply. If you are looking for an alternative to one of them, Impulse is one, with its own design and its own way of working.
